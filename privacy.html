Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Purpose

Drive Knowledge Sync is a personal utility for synchronizing Google Drive documents with a local Obsidian knowledge base.

Data Access

The application requests read-only access to Google Drive to retrieve documents selected for synchronization.

Data Storage

Retrieved documents are converted to Markdown and stored locally on the user's computer. OAuth credentials are stored locally in the rclone configuration.

Data Sharing

The application does not sell or share Google Drive user data with advertisers or other third parties. This website does not collect Google Drive documents or OAuth credentials.

Data Control

Users can remove locally synchronized data and revoke Google Drive access through their Google Account settings.

Contact

For inquiries, please visit the project repository .
